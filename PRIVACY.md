# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2025-10-07

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect user information in our game app published on the Google Play Store.

Our app only collects the necessary data to display a high score ranking. The data we may collect includes:

- Username or alias (optional)

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as email, phone number, address, location, or any other information that could personally identify the user.

## 2. Use of Data

The data collected is used exclusively to:

- Display the high score ranking within the app.

We do not share this data with third parties nor use it for commercial purposes.

## 3. Storage and Security

Scores and usernames are stored securely, using technical measures to protect the data against unauthorized access.

## 4. User Rights

The user may request the removal of their information from the ranking at any time. To do this, please contact us at cespedcompany@gmail.com.

## 6. Contact
